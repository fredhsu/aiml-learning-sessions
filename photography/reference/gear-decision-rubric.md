# `G0` — the travel-camera decision

**Time box: 3 hours total, across at most two weeks.** The learner has committed to buying a camera. When the box closes, buy the cheapest option that clears every must; there is no open-ended deferral and no search for an optimum.

This is a travel instrument, not a camera-system identity. The decision optimizes the probability that one compact kit is carried all day, becomes operational quickly, survives the planned conditions, and produces raw files that the curriculum can verify and develop. Reviews and specification comparison stop when one candidate clears the contract.

## Prerequisites and output

`G0` requires a first-pass `O7` (equivalent focal length / angle of view) and `V1` (one-clause subject identification). The entry diagnostic and `T0` still run on a phone so the purchase is informed by at least one real unfamiliar-place outing.

The Phase 0 output is a completed decision record **and a purchase or dated order**. Camera ownership is not advancement evidence; the purchase merely unblocks `G1`, raw capture, and the travel field workflow.

## Musts — a candidate that fails any is out

| Must | Why | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Records raw with intact EXIF | Verification, clipping analysis, and development depend on it | Specification plus sample raw import |
| Aperture, shutter, ISO, exposure compensation, and focus area can be changed through dials, buttons, or custom controls without main-menu diving | `G1` is a motor-fluency node; menu-only controls consume attention in changing scenes | Handle it or inspect the control map |
| Body plus mounted everyday lens fits the declared carry envelope | A technically superior camera left at lodging fails the travel outcome | Weigh the actual combination |
| Everyday lens covers approximately 24–70 mm full-frame equivalent, or the learner explicitly accepts the coverage omitted by a fixed lens | Travel practice needs orientation, context, and detail without making lens changes the default activity | Equivalent focal-length check |
| Complete working kit is within budget | Include lens, two reliable batteries total, two cards, charger/cable, and minimal weather protection | Arithmetic |
| Power can be replenished from the declared travel charging kit | Proprietary-charger dependence is acceptable only if deliberately carried | End-to-end charging test |
| Raw files open in the actual development/verification workflow | Compatibility must be established before the return window closes | Capture, ingest, inspect EXIF, open in darktable |

## Shoulds — weighted tie-breakers

| Should | Weight |
|---|---|
| Compact standard zoom available; small normal prime can be added later for a blocked constraint | High |
| Eye-level viewfinder | High; make it a must only when bright conditions, stability, or learner preference establish the need |
| Reliable autofocus with configurable subject/eye detection plus a clear single-point mode | High |
| USB-C in-camera charging or power delivery | High |
| Weather sealing when matched by a sealed lens | Medium; high only if declared conditions justify it |
| In-body or in-lens stabilisation | Medium; useful for static low-light scenes, not subject motion |
| Quiet/electronic shutter whose rolling-shutter limits are understood | Medium for unobtrusive public work |
| Two card slots | Medium for once-only or paid travel; low for local simulations if the field backup procedure is reliable |
| Comfortable operation with light gloves | Conditional on declared climate |

## Explicitly irrelevant now

- Megapixel count beyond roughly 20
- Sensor format as status; evaluate only its concrete weight, lens, depth-of-field, noise, and cost consequences
- Video features
- Brand ecosystem and hypothetical future lenses
- Laboratory score differences that do not change a must
- A long telephoto, ultrawide, tripod, flash, or second body without a demonstrated travel story that needs it
- Current generation when a supported previous-generation body clears the contract

## Default when the time box closes

Buy a **used or previous-generation APS-C or Micro Four Thirds mirrorless body with direct controls, USB charging, and a compact standard zoom roughly equivalent to 24–70 mm; prefer an eye-level viewfinder when it fits the travel contract**. If a credible all-day carry test favors a fixed lens, choose roughly 28–40 mm equivalent and record the lost coverage as a deliberate constraint. Add no second lens until two reviewed outings identify a repeated frame that the current lens could not make.

This default favors travel readiness and story coverage over maximum shallow depth of field. A small normal prime remains useful for Phase 1 blocked practice, but it is an optional later tool rather than the purchase default.

## Purchase acceptance test — within the return window

1. Carry the complete kit for two hours.
2. From powered off and packed, make an intentional frame within the declared target time.
3. Change aperture, shutter, ISO, exposure compensation, and focus area without entering the main menu.
4. Capture raw, ingest it, verify EXIF, and open it in darktable.
5. Charge from the actual travel power kit.
6. Run one low-light static scene and one moving-subject scene; distinguish stabilisation benefit from subject-motion limits.
7. Record every failure. Return the camera only for a failed must, not for buyer's remorse or a newly discovered review.
