# Travel field readiness and data stewardship

This is the operational gate for a travel simulation or real trip. It prevents avoidable failures; completing it is **not** photographic competence and does not advance a perceptual node.

## Before leaving

- **Story question:** one sentence describing what this place or journey might reveal; revise it in the field if evidence changes it.
- **Constraint:** one learning constraint for the outing; travel complexity does not suspend blocked practice.
- **Carry envelope:** body, mounted lens, one spare battery, cards, weather cover, cleaning cloth, and only accessories with a declared job; weigh the carried kit against the limit in the intent file.
- **Power and media:** charged camera and spare; cards formatted only after prior files exist in two verified copies; clock and timezone correct.
- **Access and safety:** check local restrictions, opening/transport times, weather, route, sunset, personal-security risks, and the conditions under which the camera stays packed.
- **People plan:** decide which situations require explicit consent, which allow unobtrusive public photography, and which subjects or contexts are off limits.
- **Data plan:** name two independent destinations for verified copies before any card is reused.

## On arrival

1. Spend no more than the declared orientation time identifying light direction, likely subject flow, access boundaries, and one fallback route.
2. Make the first intentional frame within the declared target unless safety, access, or ethics blocks it; log the blocker rather than rushing.
3. Track coverage roles as diagnostic prompts—orientation, context, human presence, detail, transition, closure—without treating them as a mandatory final sequence.
4. Record rejections: unsafe, prohibited, intrusive, visually empty, or wrong light. Choosing not to make a frame is evidence of judgement when the reason is recorded.

## Before leaving the location

- Confirm the story question still fits; record the revision if it changed.
- Check for one missing role that the story genuinely needs, not every role in the list.
- Verify battery/card state and that camera time is correct.
- Record any ethical-access decision that changed capture or selection.

## Weather and environment transitions

- Declare the rejection threshold before exposure to rain, dust, heat, cold, or unsafe footing; the camera staying packed is a valid result.
- On a cold-to-warm or dry-to-humid transition, keep the camera sealed until it approaches ambient temperature; do not change lenses during condensation risk.
- In rain or dust, minimize lens changes, use the declared cover, and inspect seals, ports, lens surfaces, and media before repacking.
- Log the condition, adaptation, and any failure. Premium sealing never replaces the rejection threshold.

## Before card reuse

1. Ingest originals with metadata intact.
2. Verify file count and byte integrity at destination A.
3. Copy to independent destination B and verify it.
4. Record both paths and verification time in the outing record.
5. Open a sampled file from destination B or perform the declared restore check; a copy that cannot be restored is not verified.
6. Reuse or format the card only after both checks pass.

Cloud sync may be one destination only after it reports completion and the files can be enumerated. A laptop and an attached drive count as two copies but remain one physical-loss domain; on a real trip, separate them when practical.
