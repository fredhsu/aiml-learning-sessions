# Travel-story rubric

Use this rubric for a multi-image travel edit. Score individual frames first with [`image-critique-rubric.md`](image-critique-rubric.md); a sequence can contain strong frames and still fail as a story.

## Protocol

1. Before critique, the learner commits **one predicted total for the sequence**, the expected weakest transition, and the frame most likely to be cut. Not per dimension — see the protocol note in [`image-critique-rubric.md`](image-critique-rubric.md). The weakest-transition and mandatory-cut predictions are the discrimination instrument here, and they matter more than the total.
2. The tutor reviews the ordered sequence blind to captions and stated story question where possible.
3. The story question and coverage record are revealed; the tutor separately judges whether the edit answers its own question.
4. Compute calibration gaps, identify one mandatory cut or state why none exists, and name the single missing or redundant role doing the most damage.
5. A sequence cannot pass if its ethical-integrity dimension is 0, regardless of total score.

## Anchors

Every dimension is 0–3: `0` absent or contradictory; `1` attempted but the failure dominates; `2` sound and sufficient; `3` deliberate and load-bearing.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Place specificity** | Could be anywhere | Relies on a landmark or caption | Distinctive relationships make the place legible | Place is revealed through multiple non-redundant observations |
| **Story question** | No discernible subject | Topic exists but sequence wanders | Sequence answers one clear question | Every inclusion deepens or complicates the question |
| **Coverage and omission** | Critical context absent | Roles repeat while a needed role is missing | Needed orientation/context/human/detail/transition roles are present | Omission is deliberate; no frame exists merely to complete a checklist |
| **Sequence and pacing** | Order is arbitrary | One transition breaks flow | Order creates a readable progression | Reordering any key pair weakens meaning or rhythm |
| **Visual coherence and variation** | Frames conflict without purpose | Repetition or abrupt style shifts dominate | Consistent interpretation with useful variation of scale, distance, and moment | Variation creates rhythm while the set remains unmistakably one body |
| **Edit discipline** | Obvious weak or duplicate frames remain | One indulgent/redundant inclusion | Every frame clears the story threshold | The edit is compressed to its strongest necessary form |
| **Ethical integrity** | Harmful, prohibited, deceptive, or consent failure | Defensible concern left unresolved | Capture and selection are culturally and contextually defensible | Ethical constraint actively improves distance, framing, captioning, or omission |
| **Context and caption accuracy** | Materially misleading claim, location, identity, or sequence | Context is asserted but unsupported or ambiguous | Needed facts are checked and uncertainty is stated | Precise context deepens the story without making the captions carry weak pictures |
| **Technical continuity** | Technical failures break the sequence | Inconsistent capture/development distracts | Technical choices support continuity | Technical variation is deliberate and carries the story |

## Mandatory report

- Scored blind: yes/no and why
- Predicted vs actual sequence total, signed; and whether the predicted weakest transition and predicted cut were the actual ones
- Scored per dimension by the tutor regardless, since dimension scores are what route to a node
- Story question achieved: yes/no, separately from total
- Strongest sequence break, located between named frames
- Mandatory cut, with reason
- Missing or redundant coverage role
- Ethical-access verdict and any unresolved uncertainty
- Context/caption facts checked, uncertainty, and source where one was needed
- One capture-time change for the next outing
- One editing change for the next sequence
