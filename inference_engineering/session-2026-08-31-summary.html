Key correction: reading weights is not an alternative to doing matrix multiplication; it is required to do it. At batch 1, a bf16 weight supports roughly one FMA: 2 FLOPs per 2 bytes, or about 1 FLOP/B. Low weight reuse makes decode plausibly memory-bandwidth-bound.

W0 bandwidth measurement

Contract W0 measured a 1 GiB contiguous CUDA float32 reduction with 10 warmups, 30 synchronized samples, and unlocked clocks.

MetricResult

The honest explanation for the prediction miss: no physical scale model existed before the run. The W0 value is contract-specific, not a universal claim about all RTX 4090 workloads.

Measurement discipline

  • Declare a workload contract and prediction before every measurement.
  • Report distributions, not just means.
  • Do not call a bottleneck without arithmetic or profiler evidence.
  • Do not double-count context: the 2,048-token KV cost was already in the decode-byte model.
